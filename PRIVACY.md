# Data handling notes

These notes describe the supplied source and are not a substitute for a deployment-specific privacy policy or an independent runtime/security audit. No personal device exports, signing keys, captured logs or private screenshots are included in this source package.

Games, training progress, settings and backups are stored locally by the application. Users can export PGN and backups. Peer challenges and external puzzle/tablebase services use network connections; optional speech recognition depends on the device's speech service and its permissions/policy. Sharing passes selected content to the user's chosen Android application. Network providers may receive connection metadata; no centralized account service is bundled.
